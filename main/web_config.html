Wi-Fi network

2.4 GHz only. The board cannot use 5 GHz networks.

I2S pins

GPIO numbers wired to the PCM5102A DAC. Do not use GPIO 6 to 11 (flash) or 16 and 17 (PSRAM).

Device name

The name shown in the AirPlay list on your iPhone, iPad or Mac.

Letters, numbers and hyphens only, 31 characters max.
